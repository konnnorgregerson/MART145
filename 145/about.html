Why I Enjoy Fishing

I enjoy fishing because it gives me a chance to spend time outdoors and I enjoy being around the water. Fishing can be relaxing, but it can also be exciting when the fish are biting.

What Makes Fishing Fun

Every fishing trip is different. Some days the boys just jump in the truck and go to the river. While other trips we plan ahead of time and prepare for what types of fish we are trying to catch.

My Favorite Parts of Fishing

  • Spending time outside
  • Relaxing near the water
  • Catching different types of fish
  • Trying different techniques and flys
  • Spending time with friends and family

A New Hobby I Enjoy

Fishing is a hobby that gives me a reason to get outside and explore. There is always something new to learn, whether it is a new technique, a new location, or a different type of fish.
