Fish I Enjoy Catching

One of the most exciting parts of fishing is seeing what I can catch. I think fishing is unique because each cast can provide a different outcome.

Different Types of Fish

Montana has many different species of fish. Trout are some of the fish I enjoy catching, but there are many other species that can be found throughout the state.

Fish I Like to Catch

  • Rainbow trout
  • Brown trout
  • Cutthroat trout
  • Walleye
  • Northern pike

Learning About Fish

Learning about different species can help when deciding what bait, lures, and fishing techniques to use. It can also help anglers understand where different fish are commonly found.

Visit the Montana Fish, Wildlife & Parks website to learn more about fishing and all of the fish species in Montana.
