Equipment I Use

I enjoy using the Butter Stick fly rod. It allows me to fish where ever I want, with it only being 7 feet long. I try to bring this rod with me where ever I go.

Basic Fishing Equipment

There are many different types of fishing equipment. Some of the most important items are a fishing rod, reel, line, hook size, and fly size.

Gear I Take Fishing

  • Fly rod
  • Fly reel
  • Fishing line
  • Flies
  • Fly box

Choosing the Right Gear

Different types of fishing can require different equipment. I like trying different rods, flys, and setups depending on where I am fishing and what I am trying to catch.
