Fishing in Montana

Montana has many different species of fish living in its rivers, streams, and lakes. Trout are especially popular among anglers, but Montana waters are also home to many other types of fish.

Popular Fish in Montana

Different parts of Montana provide opportunities to catch different species. Different seasons also cause for different types of fish to be caught. Some fish prefer cold rivers and streams, while others are commonly found in lakes and reservoirs.

Fish Found in Montana

  • Rainbow trout
  • Brown trout
  • Cutthroat trout
  • Brook trout
  • Walleye
  • Catfish

Learning About Montana Fish

Montana Fish, Wildlife & Parks provides information about fish species, fishing regulations, and fishing opportunities throughout the state.
